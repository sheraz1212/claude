# Glass Room — Story Outline

- **Topic:** phone / social media habit, loneliness
- **Length:** about 3:40 (6 parts)
- **Hero:** as in `references/hero.png`
- **Supporting characters:**
  - **Mom:** tan, wears round glasses
  - **Friend:** grey, wears an orange scarf
  - **Dog:** small, simple line-art
- **Locations (5):** bedroom, metro train, Mom's dining room, park, beach
- **Counter (top-left):** `SCREEN TIME`
- **The glass:** thin, cold-blue line-art panes with faint reflections.
  - They grow out of his phone's glow, one pane at a time, until they form a box around him.
  - The box moves with him wherever he goes.
  - While he is inside it, every sound from outside is muffled.
- **Colour journey:** cold blue (Parts 1-4) → first warm light (Part 5) → full warm amber (Part 6).
